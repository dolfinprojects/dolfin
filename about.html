About DOLFIN PROJECTS

Built on Quality. Driven by Innovation.

Who we are

DOLFIN PROJECTS is a construction chemicals supplier and professional application service provider based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. We combine product supply with technical guidance and on-site application so that customers can work with one dependable partner.

What we do

  • Supply construction chemicals from leading manufacturers
  • Provide waterproofing, concrete repair, injection grouting, flooring, coating and joint-treatment application services
  • Support product selection for contractors, consultants and RMC plants
  • Guide customers from site inspection to handover

How we work

We start with the problem, not the product: inspection, diagnosis, and a recommended system that suits your substrate, exposure and budget. We follow manufacturer datasheets and expect structural work to be designed or approved by qualified engineers.
