Frequently asked questions

What should I send for a product enquiry?
Product or application, approximate quantity or area, location, and project type.
Is final system selection guaranteed from the website?
No. It depends on inspection, substrate, exposure and manufacturer specifications.
Do admixture dosages come from the website?
No. Dosage depends on your mix design and trials — follow the datasheet and your mix designer.
Does structural strengthening need an engineer?
Yes. It must be designed or approved by a qualified structural engineer.
Are all cracks injectable?
No. Crack type, movement and moisture decide whether and how to inject.
Which waterproofing is right for tanks?
Use only products confirmed suitable for the intended contact conditions.
